# Cutthroat Mode

When enabled by the host, Cutthroat Mode adds competitive sabotage mechanics on top of the base game.

## Rival Spaces (Changed)

Instead of a friendly contest, landing on a Rival space lets you **steal 5 affection** from a random other player with a random character. They lose it, you gain it. A Shield Brooch on the target blocks the steal entirely.

## Jealousy Mechanic

At the end of every round, the game checks each character. If two or more players are **tied for the highest affection** with that character, all tied players **lose 2 affection** with them. This punishes neck-and-neck races and encourages you to diversify your approach or pull ahead decisively.

## Heartbreaker Letter

| | |
|---|---|
| **Cost** | 5 Charm Tokens |
| **Effect** | Reduce a chosen rival's affection with a chosen character by **8** |

Only available in the Gift Shop when Cutthroat Mode is on. Blocked by the target's Shield Brooch (consumes the shield).

## Cutthroat Strategy Tips

- **Shield Brooches are essential** — buy them early to protect your hard-earned affection.
- **Diversify your affection** across characters to minimize jealousy losses.
- **Timing matters** — save your Heartbreaker Letter for late-game when point swings matter most.
- **Rival spaces are high-reward** — stealing 5 affection is a 10-point swing.
