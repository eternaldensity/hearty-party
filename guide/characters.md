# The Characters

You'll be courting five love interests, each with their own personality and preferences. Saying the right thing earns you affection — saying the wrong thing can cost you.

---

## Sable — *Warm, nurturing, bookish*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Color** | Amber |
| **Likes** | Thoughtful gifts, deep conversation, poetry |
| **Dislikes** | Aggression, showing off |
| **Board Home** | Space 5 |

Sable responds best to **thoughtful** and **kind** gestures. She appreciates **creativity** and **patience**, but doesn't care for **bold** or **flashy** behavior.

---

## Dash — *Energetic, adventurous, competitive*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Color** | Cyan |
| **Likes** | Bold moves, action, spontaneity |
| **Dislikes** | Being ignored, indecisiveness |
| **Board Home** | Space 11 |

Dash loves **bold** and **competitive** energy. He enjoys **humor** and **flashiness**, but has no patience for those who are too **patient** or slow to act.

---

## Lux — *Glamorous, dramatic, witty*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Color** | Magenta |
| **Likes** | Flattery, grand gestures, fashion |
| **Dislikes** | Boring answers, cheapness |
| **Board Home** | Space 17 |

Lux adores **flashy** displays and **bold** confidence. They appreciate **humor** and **creativity**, but find overly **patient** approaches dull.

---

## Elm — *Quiet, artistic, mysterious*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Color** | Green |
| **Likes** | Patience, creativity, nature |
| **Dislikes** | Loud behavior, rushing |

Elm values **patience** and **creativity** above all. He appreciates **thoughtful** and **kind** words, but is strongly put off by anything **bold**, **competitive**, or **flashy**.

---

## Pip — *Cheerful, goofy, optimistic*

| | |
|---|---|
