# The Board

The board is a loop of **30 spaces** with two branch points that offer alternate paths. There are also **6 branch spaces** (3 per alternate path) for a total of 36 spaces.

## Branch Points

At two points on the board you'll be asked to choose your path:

- **Branch A** (Space 8): Take the main route through spaces 9–12, or detour through an alternate path (spaces 30–32) that rejoins at space 12.
- **Branch B** (Space 20): Take the main route through spaces 21–24, or detour through an alternate path (spaces 33–35) that rejoins at space 24.

Both routes cover the same number of spaces, but the space types differ — check the board to decide which path offers what you need.

## Space Types

There are seven types of spaces, each with a distinct color:

| Space | Color | What Happens |
|-------|-------|--------------|
| **Blank** | Gray | Nothing — you pass through safely. |
| **Charm Fountain** | Teal | Gain **2 Charm Tokens** automatically. |
| **Date Spot** | Pink | A random character appears for a dialogue encounter. Pick from 3 responses — your choice affects affection based on their preferences. |
| **Love Nest** | Red | Visit a specific character's home for a guaranteed positive encounter. Affection gain ranges from **+5 to +10** depending on your response. |
| **Gift Shop** | Gold | Browse and buy items using your Charm Tokens. You have 20 seconds to shop. |
| **Event** | Purple | A random event triggers — could be good or bad, and may affect you, everyone, or another player. |
| **Rival** | Orange | A face-off with another player. In normal mode, both players gain affection. In Cutthroat Mode, you steal from them instead. |
