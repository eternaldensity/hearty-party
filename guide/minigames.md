# Minigames

Every round includes a minigame that all connected players compete in simultaneously. A random minigame is selected each round from the pool of 8 games. You'll see a 5-second intro with the rules before it starts.

## Rewards

Your rank determines your reward:

| Rank | Affection | Charm Tokens |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| 1st | +5 (random character) | +3 |
| 2nd | +3 (random character) | +2 |
| 3rd | +2 (random character) | +1 |
| Others | +1 (random character) | +1 |

---

## Action Games

### Heart Catcher
*30 seconds*

Hearts fall from the top of the screen, colored by character. Tap or click them to catch. Each catch scores 1 point. Hearts only last 3 seconds before disappearing, and new ones spawn every 0.6 seconds.

**Strategy:** Focus on the center of the screen where you can reach hearts in any direction. Don't chase hearts near the edges — more will come.

---

### Dance Floor
*35 seconds*

Directional arrows appear on screen — up, down, left, or right. When the beat is active, tap the matching direction button. Correct hits score 1 point. Land **3 or more correct hits in a row** to earn a combo bonus (+1 extra point per hit).

Missing a beat or tapping the wrong direction breaks your combo.

**Strategy:** Accuracy over speed. A sustained combo is worth far more than frantic tapping. Wait for the beat window to open before pressing.

---

### Gift Wrap Race
*25 seconds*

A simple tap-fest. Tap the big button as fast as you can to fill your wrapping progress bar. First to reach 100% wins. It takes **40 taps** to complete. You can see all players' progress bars in real time.

**Strategy:** Pure speed. Tap with two thumbs on mobile for maximum rate.

## Trivia Games

### Love Letter Scramble
*45 seconds*

5 rounds of scrambled romantic words. Each round shows a jumbled word and a hint. Type the unscrambled word and submit. The first player to solve each word gets **3 points**. Rounds auto-advance after 8 seconds or when someone solves it.

Words include: ROMANCE, SWEETHEART, PASSION, DARLING, SERENADE, BLOSSOM, DEVOTION, CHERISH, ADORE, EMBRACE, AMOUR, DESIRE, TENDER, FLUTTER, BELOVED.

**Strategy:** Read the hint first — it narrows down the word significantly. Short words (ADORE, AMOUR) are easier to solve quickly.

---

### Sweet Nothings
*30 seconds*

5 rounds of character quotes. A quote appears and you pick which of 3 characters said it. Answering correctly within 3 seconds earns **3 points**. Correct after 3 seconds earns **1 point**. Wrong answers earn nothing.

**Strategy:** Learn the characters' speaking styles. Sable quotes are literary and gentle, Dash is action-oriented, Lux is dramatic and glamorous, Elm is quiet and nature-themed, and Pip is goofy and food-related.

---

### Compatibility Quiz
*60 seconds*

5 rounds of personality questions with a target character shown each round. Pick the answer whose personality category best matches the target character's preferences. The character's hidden preference weight for your chosen category is converted to points (0–6 per round).

For example, if the target is Elm and you pick a "creative" answer (Elm's weight: +3), you score 6 points. Picking a "flashy" answer (Elm's weight: -3) scores 0 points.

Rounds auto-advance after 10 seconds or when all players answer.

**Strategy:** This is a test of how well you know the characters. Review the [Characters](characters.md) page to understand each one's likes and dislikes.

## Strategy Games

### Perfect Match
*40 seconds*

A shared memory card game. 16 cards (8 pairs) are laid face-down, each pair themed to a character. Tap a card to flip it. Flip two cards — if they match, you score **1 point** and they stay revealed. If they don't match, they flip back after 1.5 seconds.

All players share the same board and can see each other's flips, so pay attention even when you're not flipping.

**Strategy:** Watch what other players reveal. You can capitalize on their mismatches by remembering where cards are.

---

### Rose Garden
*45 seconds*

A territory control game on a 6x6 grid. Players take turns placing one rose on an empty cell. After each round of placements, all roses **spread to adjacent empty cells** (up/down/left/right). If two different players' roses would spread to the same cell, neither claims it.

5 rounds of placement + spreading. The player with the most cells at the end wins.

**Strategy:** Place roses near the center for maximum spread potential. Avoid placing directly adjacent to opponents — your roses will contest the same cells and cancel out. Corners and edges limit spread directions.
