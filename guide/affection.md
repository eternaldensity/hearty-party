# Affection

Affection is the core scoring mechanic. You track a separate affection score (0–100) with each of the five characters.

## How You Gain (and Lose) Affection

| Source | Range | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Date Spot dialogue | -5 to +8 | Depends on the character's preference for your chosen response |
| Love Nest dialogue | +5 to +10 | Always positive — visit a character at their home |
| Minigame rewards | +1 to +5 | Based on your final ranking (applied to a random character) |
| Event spaces | -3 to +4 | Random events can help or hurt |
| Rival spaces (normal) | +2 to +4 | Friendly competition; both players gain |
| Items | Varies | Love Letter (+5), Bouquet (+3 to all), etc. |

## Date Spot Dialogue

When you land on a Date Spot, a random character appears with a scenario and three response options. Each response is tagged with a personality category (like *thoughtful*, *bold*, *funny*, etc.). The character's hidden preference for that category determines how much affection you gain or lose:

| Character's Preference | Affection Change |
|------------------------|-----------------|
| Strongly dislikes | -5 |
| Dislikes | -3 |
| Slightly dislikes | -1 |
| Neutral | +1 |
| Slightly likes | +3 |
| Likes | +5 |
| Loves | +8 |

**Tip:** Learn what each character values. Sable loves thoughtful answers while Dash craves bold ones. The same response can be a hit with one character and a miss with another.

## Milestones

Reaching **25**, **50**, and **75** affection with a character unlocks milestones — visible markers of your growing bond.
