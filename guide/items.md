# Charm Tokens & Items

**Charm Tokens** are the game's currency. You earn them from Charm Fountain spaces (+2), minigame rewards, and events.

## Gift Shop Items

| Item | Cost | Effect |
|------|------|--------|
| **Love Letter** | 3 | +5 affection with a character of your choice |
| **Bouquet** | 5 | +3 affection with ALL characters |
| **Dice Charm** | 3 | Choose any number 1–6 for your next roll |
| **Swap Scroll** | 4 | Swap board positions with another player |
| **Mirror** | 6 | Copy another player's last affection gain |
| **Shield Brooch** | 4 | Block the next negative effect targeting you |

Items are purchased at Gift Shop spaces and used from your inventory via the use-item action.

### Cutthroat-Only Item

| Item | Cost | Effect |
|------|------|--------|
| **Heartbreaker Letter** | 5 | Reduce a chosen rival's affection with a chosen character by **8** |

Only available when Cutthroat Mode is enabled. Blocked by the target's Shield Brooch (consumes the shield).
