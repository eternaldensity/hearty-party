# Tips for New Players

## General Strategy

- **Pay attention to character preferences.** Each character has strong likes and dislikes. Picking the right response at a Date Spot can mean the difference between +8 and -5.
- **Love Nests are always positive.** Landing on a character's home space always gives you +5 to +10 affection — there's no way to lose here.
- **Save tokens for key items.** A well-timed Love Letter (+5 to your best character) or Bouquet (+3 to all) can swing the game.
- **Branch paths matter.** Check what spaces are on each path before choosing — a Gift Shop or Love Nest might be worth the detour.
- **Watch the standings.** Keep an eye on who's leading and with which characters.

## Minigame Tips

- **Minigames happen every round** — don't neglect them. The +5 affection and +3 tokens for 1st place add up over 15 rounds.
- **Learn the trivia games.** Sweet Nothings and Compatibility Quiz reward character knowledge. Read the [Characters](characters.md) guide to learn their personalities.
- **Action games reward raw speed.** Heart Catcher and Gift Wrap Race are purely mechanical — practice your tapping.
- **Strategy games reward patience.** In Perfect Match, watching other players' failed matches gives you free information. In Rose Garden, center placement beats edge placement.

## Confession Strategy

Going for a Confession win (100 affection with one character) is extremely risky but rewarding:

- **Focus all efforts on one character.** Visit their Love Nest whenever possible, buy Love Letters targeting them, and always pick their preferred dialogue responses.
- **You need roughly +7 affection per round** to hit 100 in 15 rounds. That requires near-perfect dialogue choices plus item and minigame bonuses.
- **Other players can't directly stop you** in normal mode, but in Cutthroat Mode, Heartbreaker Letters and jealousy mechanics make this strategy much harder.
- **It's all-or-nothing.** If you don't reach 100, your total affection across all characters will likely be low since you neglected four of them.
