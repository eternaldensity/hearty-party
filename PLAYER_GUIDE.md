# Hearty Party — Player Guide

Hearty Party is a multiplayer board game with a dating sim twist. 2–8 players join on their phones or PCs, roll dice, move around a board, land on spaces that trigger events, compete in minigames, and try to win the hearts of five charming characters. A typical game runs about 15 rounds.
