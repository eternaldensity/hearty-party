# Hearty Party — Player Guide

Hearty Party is a multiplayer board game with a dating sim twist. 2–8 players join on their phones or PCs, roll dice, move around a board, land on spaces that trigger events, and compete to win the hearts of five charming characters. A typical game runs about 15 rounds.

---

## Getting Started

1. One player **creates a game** and shares the 4-letter room code.
2. Other players **join** by entering the code and a display name.
3. The host configures settings and presses **Start Game** when everyone is in.

### Game Settings

| Setting | Default | Description |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| Total Rounds | 15 | How many rounds the game lasts |
| Cutthroat Mode | Off | Enables stealing, sabotage, and jealousy mechanics |

---

## How to Win

At the end of all rounds, the player with the **highest total affection** across all five characters wins.

Your total affection is the sum of your individual affection scores with each character (each ranging from 0 to 100).

## The Board

The board is a loop of **30 spaces** with two branch points that offer alternate paths. There are also **6 branch spaces** (3 per alternate path) for a total of 36 spaces.

### Branch Points

At two points on the board you'll be asked to choose your path:

- **Branch A** (Space 8): Take the main route through spaces 9–12, or detour through an alternate path (spaces 30–32) that rejoins at space 12.
- **Branch B** (Space 20): Take the main route through spaces 21–24, or detour through an alternate path (spaces 33–35) that rejoins at space 24.

Both routes cover the same number of spaces, but the space types differ — check the board to decide which path offers what you need.

### Space Types

There are seven types of spaces, each with a distinct color:

| Space | Color | What Happens |
|-------|-------|--------------|
| **Blank** | Gray | Nothing — you pass through safely. |
| **Charm Fountain** | Teal | Gain **2 Charm Tokens** automatically. |
| **Date Spot** | Pink | A random character appears for a dialogue encounter. Pick from 3 responses — your choice affects affection based on their preferences. |
| **Love Nest** | Red | Visit a specific character's home for a guaranteed positive encounter. Affection gain ranges from **+5 to +10** depending on your response. |
| **Gift Shop** | Gold | Browse and buy items using your Charm Tokens. You have 20 seconds to shop. |
| **Event** | Purple | A random event triggers — could be good or bad, and may affect you, everyone, or another player. |
| **Rival** | Orange | A face-off with another player. In normal mode, both players gain affection. In Cutthroat Mode, you steal from them instead. |

## Round Flow

Each round follows this sequence:

1. **Round Start** — The round number is displayed.
2. **Dice Phase** — All players roll simultaneously (tap/click to roll). If you don't roll within 3 seconds, the game rolls for you. Roll range: **1–6**.
3. **Move Phase** — Players move one at a time. If you hit a branch point, you choose which path to take (10-second timer, defaults to the main path).
4. **Space Phase** — Each player's landed space activates in order. You might get a dialogue choice, open a shop, or trigger an event.
5. **Round End** — Standings are shown, then the next round begins.

After all rounds are complete, the game ends and final standings are revealed.

## Affection

Affection is the core scoring mechanic. You track a separate affection score (0–100) with each of the five characters.

### How You Gain (and Lose) Affection

| Source | Range | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Date Spot dialogue | -5 to +8 | Depends on the character's preference for your chosen response |
| Love Nest dialogue | +5 to +10 | Always positive — visit a character at their home |
| Event spaces | -3 to +4 | Random events can help or hurt |
| Rival spaces (normal) | +2 to +4 | Friendly competition; both players gain |
| Items | Varies | Love Letter (+5), Bouquet (+3 to all), etc. |

### Date Spot Dialogue

When you land on a Date Spot, a random character appears with a scenario and three response options. Each response is tagged with a personality category (like *thoughtful*, *bold*, *funny*, etc.). The character's hidden preference for that category determines how much affection you gain or lose:

| Character's Preference | Affection Change |
|------------------------|-----------------|
| Strongly dislikes | -5 |
| Dislikes | -3 |
| Slightly dislikes | -1 |
| Neutral | +1 |
| Slightly likes | +3 |
| Likes | +5 |
| Loves | +8 |

**Tip:** Learn what each character values. Sable loves thoughtful answers while Dash craves bold ones. The same response can be a hit with one character and a miss with another.

### Milestones

Reaching **25**, **50**, and **75** affection with a character unlocks milestones — visible markers of your growing bond.

## Charm Tokens & Items

**Charm Tokens** are the game's currency. You earn them from Charm Fountain spaces (+2), events, and minigames.

### Gift Shop Items

| Item | Cost | Effect |
|------|------|--------|
| **Love Letter** | 3 | +5 affection with a character of your choice |
| **Bouquet** | 5 | +3 affection with ALL characters |
| **Dice Charm** | 3 | Choose any number 1–6 for your next roll |
| **Swap Scroll** | 4 | Swap board positions with another player |
| **Mirror** | 6 | Copy another player's last affection gain |
| **Shield Brooch** | 4 | Block the next negative effect targeting you |

Items are purchased at Gift Shop spaces and used from your inventory at any time via the use-item action.

## Random Events

Landing on an Event space triggers one of these random outcomes:

| Event | Effect | Who's Affected |
|-------|--------|----------------|
| Found a shiny coin | +2 tokens | You |
| Warm breeze carries sweet words | +3 affection (random character) | You |
| Tripped in front of everyone | -2 affection (random character) | You |
| Flower petals rain from the sky | +2 affection (random character) | Everyone |
| Mysterious benefactor gives a gift | +1 token | Everyone |
| Someone ate another player's snack | -2 affection (random character) | A random other player |
| Secret admirer note | +4 affection (random character) | You |
| Lucky day — extra tokens | +3 tokens | You |
| Someone spread gossip about you | -3 affection (random character) | You |
| Group photo — smiles all around | +1 affection (random character) | Everyone |
| Dropped your wallet | -2 tokens | You |
| Impromptu dance party | +2 affection (random character) | Everyone |

**Note:** The Shield Brooch blocks negative *affection* effects (like gossip or tripping), but not token losses.

## Cutthroat Mode

When enabled by the host, Cutthroat Mode adds competitive sabotage mechanics on top of the base game.

### Rival Spaces (Changed)

Instead of a friendly contest, landing on a Rival space (spaces 10 and 22) lets you **steal 5 affection** from a random other player with a random character. They lose it, you gain it. A Shield Brooch on the target blocks the steal entirely.

### Jealousy Mechanic

At the end of every round, the game checks each character. If two or more players are **tied for the highest affection** with that character, all tied players **lose 2 affection** with them. This punishes neck-and-neck races and encourages you to diversify your approach or pull ahead decisively.

### Heartbreaker Letter (Cutthroat-Only Item)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Cost** | 5 Charm Tokens |
| **Effect** | Reduce a chosen rival's affection with a chosen character by **8** |

Only available in the Gift Shop when Cutthroat Mode is on. Blocked by the target's Shield Brooch (consumes the shield).

### Cutthroat Strategy Tips

- **Shield Brooches are essential** — buy them early to protect your hard-earned affection.
- **Diversify your affection** across characters to minimize jealousy losses.
- **Timing matters** — save your Heartbreaker Letter for late-game when point swings matter most.
- **Rival spaces are high-reward** — stealing 5 affection is a 10-point swing.

---

## Tips for New Players

- **Pay attention to character preferences.** Each character has strong likes and dislikes. Picking the right response at a Date Spot can mean the difference between +8 and -5.
- **Love Nests are always positive.** Landing on a character's home space always gives you +5 to +10 affection — there's no way to lose here.
- **Save tokens for key items.** A well-timed Love Letter (+5 to your best character) or Bouquet (+3 to all) can swing the game.
- **Branch paths matter.** Check what spaces are on each path before choosing — a Gift Shop or Love Nest might be worth the detour.
- **Watch the standings.** Keep an eye on who's leading and with which characters. In Cutthroat Mode, being tied for first with a character costs you every round.
